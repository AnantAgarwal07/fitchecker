## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-fashion digital salon: an uncompromising convergence of classical editorial luxury and streamlined social engagement. Targeted at tastemakers, fashion curators, and style-conscious digital natives, the atmosphere is deliberate, refined, and tactile.

Rooted in a contemporary high-fashion editorial aesthetic, the design pairs extreme typographic contrast with expansive negative space and warm, parchment-tinted surfaces. The interface recedes to let visual media take center stage while preserving an authoritative, bespoke character through meticulously balanced typography and controlled violet accents.

## Colors

The palette establishes an haute couture environment using warm, museum-grade light surfaces accented with electric imperial violet.

- **Primary (`#7C3AED`)**: The anchor accent for primary actions, verified badges, active curation states, and focal moments.
- **Secondary (`#A78BFA`)**: A softer violet tone utilized for secondary interaction states, decorative micro-indicators, and ambient icon fills.
- **Tertiary (`#EDE9FE`)**: A tinted lavender mist dedicated to badge backgrounds, subtle active state chips, and soft selection washes.
- **Neutral (`#18181B`)**: Deepest velvet charcoal serving as the dominant text and outline color, providing decisive editorial contrast against backgrounds.
- **Surface Roles**: Canvas surfaces use warm alabaster `#FAF8F5`, elevated card surfaces sit at `#FFFFFF`, and subtle dividing borders utilize `#E8E5DF`.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy rests upon the juxtaposition of Bodoni Moda and Manrope. 

- **Bodoni Moda** delivers dramatic editorial impact with its high stroke contrast and refined vertical stress. It is reserved exclusively for major focal headings, mastheads, discovery headers, and lookbook volume titles.
- **Manrope** provides a disciplined geometric counterweight. Its open counters and geometric clarity ensure immediate legibility across social engagement metrics, user handles, product tags, captions, and dense metadata feeds.
- Display and headline levels dynamically adapt between mobile and wider viewport contexts to maintain vertical rhythm without unintended text clipping.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural model is tailored for immersive vertical feeds with seamless mobile portrait navigation, scaling out to structured columns on larger canvases.

- **Mobile Canvas**: A singular vertical column layout flanked by a 16px (`1rem`) outer margin. Media elements, such as fashion lookbooks and full-bleed stories, may break standard margins to achieve edge-to-edge bleed.
- **Desktop & Tablet Form Factors**: Expands into a centered multi-column grid (up to 12 columns, max-width 1200px) with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and fixed 32px (`2rem`) outer margins.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm**: Micro-elements and metadata rely on `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px). Card inner padding relies on `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px). Section dividers and feed item breaks use `space-xl` (40px) to sustain an airy magazine publication cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

The design system rejects heavy drop shadows in favor of ambient luminescence and low-contrast surface demarcations.

- **Level 0 (Flat)**: Baseline canvas surface (`#FAF8F5`) without shadows.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Boundaries)**: Surfaces are elevated via crisp 1px borders in `#E8E5DF` combined with a soft ambient veil: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Action Bars & Overlays)**: Floating bottom navigation pills, sticky headers, and quick-action sheets utilize a composite treatment: backdrop blur of 16px (`blur(16px)`), a semi-translucent base `#FAF8F5` at 85% opacity, and a low-opacity shadow tinted with violet: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Sheets)**: Centered modals and expanded lookbook carousels leverage `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.12)` accompanied by an ambient backdrop scrim.

## Shapes

Shapes follow a rounded radius standard (Level 2), harmonizing architectural precision with ergonomic hand feel:

- **Standard Elements**: Interactive cards, input fields, and standard containers adopt `0.5rem` (`rounded-md` equivalent).
- **Emphasized Containers**: Post cards, editorial reels, and discovery viewports scale up to `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) and `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`).
- **Interactive Micro-Components**: Search inputs, tag badges, and floating control pills utilize fully continuous rounded geometry (`rounded-full`) to contrast against rectangular editorial photography.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#18181B` fill with `#FFFFFF` text in Manrope SemiBold, or primary `#7C3AED` for key conversion steps. Radius of `0.5rem` or full pill. Padding: 12px 24px.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Ghost borders with 1px `#E8E5DF` over `#FFFFFF`, shifting to `#EDE9FE` wash with `#7C3AED` text upon interaction.
- **Floating Action Pills**: High-blur translucent pills (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.9)`) housing 20px monochrome icons with violet state highlights.

### Chips & Badges
- **Curated Style Badges**: Background `#EDE9FE`, foreground `#7C3AED`, padding 4px 10px, radius full pill, typography `label-sm` in all-caps tracking.
- **Category Chips**: Inactive state features `#FFFFFF` background with 1px `#E8E5DF` border; active state transitions to `#18181B` background with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Post Cards & Lookbook Tiles
- **Editorial Post Card**: `#FFFFFF` background with a 1px border `#E8E5DF` and radius `1rem`. Houses a 4:5 vertical media frame with top-aligned author meta and bottom caption overlay.
- **Tagging Overlay**: Interactive floating hotspot pins placed directly on imagery, displaying a clean translucent pill displaying product title and pricing upon tap.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Search & Filter Bars**: Pill-shaped containers (`rounded-full`) filled with `#FFFFFF`, bordered by `#E8E5DF`, featuring placeholder text in `#71717A` and active cursor in `#7C3AED`.
- **Text Inputs**: Flat `#FFFFFF` fields with 1px border `#E8E5DF`, corner radius `0.5rem`, height 48px, focusing cleanly to a 1.5px `#7C3AED` outline without default browser ring offset.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 20px height and width, `#E8E5DF` default border. Active state fills with `#7C3AED` displaying a crisp white micro-check or inner dot.

### Navigation Tabs
- **Bottom Navigation Bar**: Suspended floating bar (`margin: 16px`, radius `1.5rem`), backed by 20px blur glassmorphism, holding five core navigational icons: Home, Trending, Camera/Create, For You, Profile. Active tab indicated by a subtle `#7C3AED` dot below the icon.
